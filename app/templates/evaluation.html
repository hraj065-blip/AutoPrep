THE AGENT’S VALUE ADDITION

Equal accuracy, more useful decisions

On this controlled suite, the agent and fixed trim baseline both reach 100% exact-cell accuracy. The agent’s distinctive result is that it identifies duplicate rows as a consequential decision and routed all 10 duplicate cases to review. A no-op or fixed trim script has no review workflow.

This is not proof of broad superiority: the suite has 100 small synthetic cases, exactly 10 per family, and only one safe automatic transformation. Review labels and outcomes are specific to this benchmark.
